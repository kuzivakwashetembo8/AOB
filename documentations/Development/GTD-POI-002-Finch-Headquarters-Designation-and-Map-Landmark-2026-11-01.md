## 2. Approved building concept; deferred construction

Only **two accessible, fully designed levels** are proposed in this tower:

1. **Ground level:** a purpose-designed arrival/reception room reached through the existing street entrance, plus an elevator at the building's edge.
2. **Floor 53 (highest occupied floor):** a purpose-designed meeting/operations room in which Finch is present, with an elevator exit and outward views over Meridia.

**Floors 2–52:** No furniture, accessible room content, hallways, staircases, or full interior level layout required. They remain visually represented by the existing procedural facade/slab structure but cannot be reached via gameplay. The elevator will ultimately transfer Voight between two valid physical floors without rendering or simulating traversal of the intermediate storeys. The elevator shaft/interior and transition design are **future decisions**.

The existing `Finch.glb` at `public/GLBs/Characters/Finch.glb` was confirmed committed to `main` in the source-tree inventory. **Do not load/position/animate Finch yet.**

Do not implement rooms, furniture, elevator controls, floor transitions, interaction logic, Finch placement, new NPC animations, missions, physics, or navigation as part of this location-marking stage.
