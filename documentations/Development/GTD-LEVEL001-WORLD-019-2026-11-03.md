## Accepted production baseline

PR #84 merged and production deployed READY. Its seven Reese/Finch fixes remain unchanged in `main`. This branch contains ONLY subsequent five follow-up changes.

## 1. Receptionist

The seated office NPC was placed at the suit GLB's bounding-box bottom minus 0.45 m, positioning its pelvis below the generated white slab. Change the resting model-origin offset to +0.15m above the lobby surface and center it on the actual seat, without repositioning or enlarging the desk. Acceptance: the receptionist is visibly **sitting on** the seat, neither sunken nor floating.

## 2. Square map

Retain the live game view behind the map with a dark 40% overlay and 4px backdrop blur. Replace the 980px-wide blue rectangular panel with a sharply focused, bounded, centered 1:1 aspect-ratio SVG map. Header and footer use the same map width; no giant blue sidebars. Preserve pan/zoom, road/roof rendering, player and anonymous mission POI.

## 3. Sidewalk curvature

Previous `roundedJunctionPavement` rounded all four corners of every intersection's quadrant, scalloping inward into the road and creating concrete blocks at edges. Generate a curve **only** at each quadrant's outer land-facing corner (x+sx*OUTER,z+sz*OUTER), preserving the three road-facing/connecting corners uncut. Remove the extra `pad(batch.curb,...,0,...)` and duplicate comments; the actual curb fascia alone extends to terrain. The visible and physical walkable surfaces remain aligned.

## 4. Diane: genuine surveillance and traffic deadlock

**Root cause confirmed:** NH012 AI's lane movement contained `if(!t&&edge.length-agent.s<=.06&&v<.5)`, where `t` is the targeted route. Diane's target car never acquired `arrivalTick` and `waitTicks` at intermediate intersections, hence it was *never eligible for intersection reservations*, independent of actual traffic occupancy. The fix allows a targeted car to join the queue at any **intermediate** stop edge while excluding its final destination. The existing reservation consistency and solid vehicle collision rules remain.

Diane begins in the eastern/far sector `{x:188,z:-230}` rather than beside Finch `{x:70,z:-58}`. Road geometry remains genuine directed-lane routing across the city. Speed adjusts between 5.8 and 8.2m/s rather than falling to an unnatural 1.5m/s when Reese is distant. After Diane's drive starts, a tail closer than 18m for at least 0.8s fails the mission; Diane flees at 4.6m/s, skips the officers meeting and persists the `failed` checkpoint. Reese must return to Finch for a short two-line failure debrief; he can then begin again from the distant starting location. Finch only reports success when the meeting and reboarding actually occurred.

**Gameplay acceptance:** Diane's car is nowhere near Finch HQ at spawn; following in a separate car requires a safe distance; target car turns through several intersections and reaches the meeting, then Diane meets officers and reboards. A reckless near-tail triggers a visible failure with Diane running and explicit return-to-Finch objective. Saves and former checkpoints remain parseable.

## 5. Elevators in every multi-storey building

The previous `finchShaft004` was applied exclusively to Finch HQ. A new generic `northHeightsBuildingShaft019` is derived from each building's stable lot identifier, floor count, floor height, width and depth; Finch continues using its existing 53-floor shaft. All visual upper floor slabs, podium rings and Rapier floor slabs call the same `finchFloorRing004` geometry with the generic shaft so collisions and geometry agree.

Static glass shaft sides/back are instanced into existing material batches. A single lazy glass cabin `createNorthHeightsBuildingLifts019` is created on entry to a non-Finch lift. It moves the player capsule, matching collision deck, camera-constrained glass box and visible carriage together, with a small bounded travel duration dependent on floor distance. The React keypad displays 1…N buttons drawn from the selected building's generated floor count. Finch's existing glass lift now also accepts any intermediate floor 1…53 while preserving its default 1↔53 behavior. The button is independent of movement controls, positioned center-right like the accepted lift controls.

**Performance/physics constraints:** do not load hundreds of individually animated elevators. Glass structure stays batched; only one cabin is active at a time, disposed on leaving/scene teardown. All permanent structural ring colliders require validation for loading overhead, count, and camera bounds on tall buildings. Verify roof height after player exits upper floors. Do not accidentally make traversable elevator shafts into freely fallable pits.
