## 1. Owner intent and guiding principle

> The Sedans already know how to move based on North Heights traffic rules. Rather than invent an entirely separate driving mechanism, let player input gradually replace the selected autonomous decisions. The first milestone is entering a moving Sedan without driving it: Voight disappears, the camera follows that very Sedan, and traffic logic continues unaltered. Add braking, then acceleration, then direction selection in separately evaluated stages.

**Fundamental architecture:** There is **one NH-012 logical Sedan agent** identified by its immutable `agent.id`, **one authoritative lane/traffic simulator**, and **one accepted GEN-VEH-009 rendering pipeline**. A rider session attaches to an existing agent; it does not replace that agent with a newly built GLB or Rapier actor.

For the initial stages, the 125 logical agents remain 125 and all 125 continue using the accepted native extracted Sedan presentation; no diagnostic dots or unapproved vehicle types. A boarded Sedan is still the same traffic car, at the same location, with the same paint and four source wheel pairs.

Distinguish **driver decisions** from **safety/road rules**. Buttons may submit a requested action to the simulator; they do not repeal road geometry, vehicle spacing, junction reservation, or collision safety. The central simulator remains responsible for final motion. The proposed input layer is **not** the South Ward `FirstDriveTrial` rigidbody controller and must not produce duplicate simulation.

### Observed gaps to resolve

1. The existing `toggleVehicle()` and nearby vehicle HUD query enumerate only `cars`, the separately parked `FirstDriveTrial` collection. Traffic agents do not enter that collection.
2. `NH012TrafficSnapshot` supplies the moving cars' `x`, `z`, `heading`, `speed` and state but no Rapier rigidbody or independent scene node. The ground-relative render pose must be derived using the **same** accepted road-contact sample as the renderer, rather than assumed to be the same as a physics collider's Y coordinate.
3. The renderer declares `update(snapshot, exclude, camera)` but presently ignores `exclude`. **Stage 1 must not use exclusion or materialize an alternate visual representation.** The same original traffic thin instance remains onscreen.
4. Traffic currently has no driver-input ownership contract, no per-agent brake/accelerator request, and no player junction-selection request.
5. Both the 125-agent simulator and the 6 parked-vehicle system must remain concurrently supported. A discriminated active-vehicle session should route controls without disturbing parked `FirstDriveTrial` behaviour.
6. The current pedestrian physics capsule must not continue normal on-foot movement or participate in an unintended collision while Voight is riding an autonomous moving Sedan. Define rider suspension and safe restoration explicitly.

## 3. Target state, invariants and boundaries

### Required invariants across all stages

- **125 logical traffic agents, no more and no fewer.** Retain their IDs, 20 Hz tick, deterministic initialization seed `120125`, directed lane graph, original following and junction safety, and established colour mapping `sedanPaint009(agent.id)`.
- **No duplicate vehicle ownership.** A ridden Sedan remains in `NH012TrafficSimulator.agents`; it is not mirrored by `FirstDriveTrial` or converted into a parked vehicle.
- **No re-extraction, reimport, GLB swap, wheel pivot rebuild, or fallback car mesh** for the selected Sedan. Original GEN-VEH-009 hub locations, glass and paint remain authoritative.
- **Visual continuity.** Entry and exit must not reset speed, pose, route progress, heading, tyre rotation, render tier, colour, or model. A quick boarding action must not display a detached wheel or a duplicate car.
- **Third-person only**, with normal touch orbit/camera distance controls and camera/world collision constraints.
- **Existing driving UI/settings parity.** Reuse the known F/vehicle action and keyboard/mobile controls; avoid adding district-specific settings for basic movement.
- **Proximity regardless of whether the vehicle is currently moving or stopped.** Apply the same bounded range and unobstructed access checks to moving and parked cars; do not silently impose a stationary-only boarding rule. Do not board through building walls or from distant rooftops.
- **No teleport to original boarding point on exit.** Exits occur at the current selected vehicle location, only when its speed is below the safe threshold and there is an actual unoccupied, grounded, reachable exit position.
- **No alteration to the six independently parked/drivable GLBs, their physics, or their controls.**
- **Preserve mobile performance and DDR/LOD.** No per-traffic-agent GLB imports, no 125 Rapier chassis, no new full-detail render object for each traffic car, and no unconditional per-frame allocation loops.
- **No unapproved NH-OPT-011 code cherry-pick.** Use prior failure documentation as a warning, not an implementation base.

### Modes

The rider/control state should be distinct from the existing on-foot `mode = "walk" | "fly"` and from existing `driving: FirstDriveTrial | null`. The first two concepts must not be conflated:

- **On foot:** Voight walks/flies normally; traffic follows AI.
- **Parked GLB driving:** existing `FirstDriveTrial` continues unmodified.
- **Sedan passenger/autopilot:** player is hidden, camera targets selected `agent.id`; traffic AI fully authoritative.
- **Sedan assisted manual:** player submits brake/throttle/next-turn requests progressively, while NH-012 remains the authoritative motion and safety system.

Suggested application-level discriminated session (design sketch only):

```ts
type ActiveVehicleSession =
  | { kind: "none" }
  | { kind: "parked-glb"; id: string; actor: FirstDriveTrial }
  | {
      kind: "traffic-sedan";
      agentId: string;
      controlStage: 1 | 2 | 3 | 4;
      mode: "autopilot" | "assisted";
    };
```

The above is an illustrative contract, not approved production code. Prefer a small adapter or state machine rather than rewriting the established six-car interaction implementation.

## 4. Stage 1 — Enter as passenger: automatic traffic driving

**Owner-approved design intent:** Voight approaches **any of the 125 moving Sedans** and activates the established vehicle action. Voight disappears from the third-person game scene; the camera changes to a follow view of **that same Sedan**. Its autonomous traffic behaviour stays unchanged. It continues driving around the city and responding to roads, cars, stops and junctions **as though nobody had entered it**. No manual steering, throttle, or brake yet.

### 4.1 Entry selection and eligibility

- Compute the selected traffic candidate using the latest authoritative `NH012TrafficSnapshot`, keyed by `agent.id`, not a Babylon thin-instance index; those indices can change with paint buckets, camera tier and culling.
- Candidate pose = `(agent.x, sampleTrafficVehicleRoadPose013(...).y, agent.z)`. A moving candidate remains eligible at the **instant** F/vehicle input is processed if within `VEHICLE_BOARD_RADIUS` (presently 3.5 m), acceptable vertical difference, no blocking building envelope and actor is genuinely on foot.
- Search both moving traffic agents and separately parked drivable cars. Choose the closest eligible candidate with deterministic tie-break by ID; respect existing parked-car selection behaviour where distances are equal. Never select hidden/far cars outside reach merely because they exist in the simulation.
- Entry is triggered once per explicit action (no repeated auto-boarding on a held button); fail safely if selected agent is missing or failed eligibility recheck between input and state transition.
- No speed-zero prerequisite. A pedestrian beside a moving Sedan can enter when proximity/access criteria are met. Camera and rider session attach without halting, teleporting or resetting traffic.
- **No body/door/seat pose system.** The accepted temporary convention remains: hide both Voight visual variants while riding. Do not revive the failed curated seating branch.

### 4.2 Session, avatar and physics

- Persist `agentId` in an active rider-session field for the duration of travel. Assert exactly one rider session and no simultaneously active parked `FirstDriveTrial` driving session.
- Hide current Voight rendered model(s) and suspend on-foot movement, jump, flying and locomotion animation updates while riding. Ensure the pedestrian Rapier capsule is not left actively obstructing traffic or misused as the moving Sedan's collider; use an explicit reversible inert/suspended rider policy compatible with the shared `MeridianPhysics` world, with no second player capsule.
- The traffic simulator remains the only moving agent authority. Read the current Sedan pose every simulation/render update. Keep UI position/minimap/location and vehicle speed tied to the Sedan while aboard, not the last pedestrian coordinates.
- Do not insert a Sedan mesh into the scene and do not remove its original thin instance. Where the selected car enters a camera frustum, existing renderer LOD and original source geometry remain in control; ensure the chase view keeps the selected Sedan visibly detailed and correctly culled.

### 4.3 Third-person follow camera

- Upon boarding, change from on-foot framing to third-person follow behind the selected traffic Sedan, using its heading and ground-sampled pose.
- Reuse `resolveNorthHeightsThirdPersonCamera`, `clampNorthHeightsPitch`, ground and building obstacle rules; preserve sensitivity and user-managed camera distance.
- On each tick or smoothly interpolated render frame, aim at the current Sedan; avoid an abrupt fixed heading snap on every AI turn. Preserve drag-to-look; when no active drag, smoothly follow heading deltas with wrap-safe angular interpolation.
- Do **not** turn the camera first-person, clip below the map, snap below an uphill roadway, attach to a wheel node, or make camera motion alter the traffic agent's heading.
- Do not silently change the accepted autonomous routing, speed, 20 Hz stepping, wheel spin, road grade compensation or 125-agent visual counts.

### 4.4 Exit, feedback and safe restoration

- F/vehicle action while riding **requests an exit**. It succeeds only when current agent speed is effectively stopped (initial accepted benchmark: absolute speed ≤0.25 m/s), and an appropriate current-world exit slot is unblocked.
- Stage 1 has no manual braking. Therefore Voight can exit at a real traffic stop, or remain riding until a valid stopping opportunity. Show clear UI feedback while moving: "Wait until the Sedan stops before exiting."
- Compute exit position from **current** Sedan pose, width/length and direction. Check roadway/curb support and nearby buildings/vehicles using existing Rapier world queries or equivalent validated clearance—not the `currentVehicleDisembark` method as-is, because that method requires a `FirstDriveTrial` with a Rapier car collider that the traffic Sedan intentionally lacks.
- On success restore Voight's physical capsule, ground support, third-person actor, on-foot context and input arbiter at the validated exit location. Keep the Sedan in AI traffic without route/speed reset. If no exit exists, remain aboard and report obstruction.
- Do not restore the character at its original boarding position and do not teleport the car to a parking lot.

### 4.5 Stage 1 tests and visual acceptance

Automated:
- Every one of the 125 IDs can be selected when in range, independent of AI `state` or positive speed.
- No out-of-range, blocked-line, height-incompatible or duplicate boarding.
- A boarded agent's 20 Hz traffic trajectory and deterministic simulation outcomes remain identical to an unboarded control run over an identical seed/input-free tick sequence.
- Stable ID through reroute, turns, paint bucket changes, camera tier changes and multiple boarding cycles.
- Original 125 IDs, 12-paint palette, source glass, 4 rim/tire pairs, 0 dots, and no temporary 126th Sedan actor.
- Safe exit only at stopped current position; no exit inside building/intersecting car/unsupported ground. Rider physics resumes exactly once.
- Parked six remain boardable and drivable as before.

Manual:
- Board a **moving** Sedan from pavement using F on desktop and vehicle button on Android; Voight vanishes and third-person camera follows that same car automatically around North Heights.
- Observe actual lane driving, cornering, wheel spin, hills, stop lines, following traffic and junctions without changes.
- Test AI stop, unsafe exit refusal, safe exit at current location, re-entry, camera orbit, minimap/vehicle HUD and mobile performance.
- **Owner must accept this standalone passenger/autopilot milestone before Stage 2.**

Deliverable: A fully usable ride-along preview with manual driving deliberately unavailable.

## 5. Stage 2 — Manual stopping and resuming

**Owner-approved design intent:** Introduce the first manual driving command: **braking**. Voight remains in the same Sedan and retains the same camera. The selected Sedan ordinarily continues its autonomous journey; while the player holds the brake input, it gradually slows to a complete halt. On release, the existing AI can resume driving when normal traffic safety permits.

### 5.1 Input contract

- Use the existing **Down Arrow / mobile Down driving button** as the initial brake input; equivalent existing desktop W/S or gamepad mappings should be wired through the common driving input abstraction, not bespoke North Heights controls.
- While riding the Stage 2 Sedan, interpret Down as **brake-to-zero only**. **Do not enable reverse** in Stage 2. Preserve parked GLB semantics in their own mode.
- Button release explicitly clears the manual brake request. A held brake dominates AI acceleration until released.
- Preserve keyboard/touch `keyup`, pointer release, pause, blur and lost capture state reset, ensuring a stuck brake is not retained unintentionally unless the player is still actively holding the control.
- On-screen guidance should distinguish "Autopilot" vs "Hold Down to brake"; do not imply unrestricted driving at this stage.

### 5.2 Simulator-owned speed override

- Introduce a narrowly scoped `Map<agentId, SedanDriverRequest>` or a single owned control record in NH-012, keyed to the selected traffic agent. There must be exactly **one** controlling rider and no global braking of the other 124 vehicles.
- Compute safe AI movement in the same simulator step, then apply the player's bounded brake request **before committing the selected agent's updated speed/distance**. Use existing `BRAKE = 3.5 m/s²` initially for traffic-consistent deceleration unless tests justify a separately documented tuning change.
- Brake target is nonnegative zero; speed monotonically approaches zero and never becomes negative. No instant 8-to-0 teleport, no hidden backward movement and no additional physics actor.
- The selected agent keeps its lane `edgeId`/`connectorId`/`s`, destination, heading and original steering/road-contact renderer. All cars behind it must recognize its actual longitudinal position and reduced speed through existing following-gap logic.
- Apply brake while on either an ordinary directed edge or a junction connector. If halted in a connector, keep its conflict reservation; do not release or transfer the reservation while physically occupying the junction.
- On brake release, remove the override and use ordinary NH-012 acceleration/following, safe stop-speed and junction reservation logic. A traffic jam, blocked lane or occupied junction must continue to hold the car even after release.
- Traffic braking state/progress telemetry must distinguish ordinary AI braking and player override for diagnostics without breaking `NH012MotionState` consumers.

### 5.3 Special cases and validation

- Braking while another car follows, while the ridden car follows a leader, approaching stop line, within a turning connector, and on uphill/downhill roads.
- Once fully stopped and exit clearance is validated, F exits the current Sedan (Stage 1 policy).
- Explicit handling for a player indefinitely blocking an intersection: no silent teleport or car deletion; keep correct reservation and allow recovery by releasing brake.
- Test 20 Hz deterministic trajectories with identical timed brake inputs, speed clamp, reservation integrity and car-spacing invariants; test mobile press/release and no stuck button.
- Compare performance and accepted wheel geometry with Stage 1. Do not change the renderer for braking.
- **Owner acceptance required before Stage 3.**

Deliverable: Player can stop and release **their selected** autonomous Sedan while retaining all traffic protections.

## 6. Stage 3 — Manual acceleration / assisted speed control

**Owner-approved design intent:** Add accelerator control. The player's requested acceleration determines how quickly the selected Sedan proceeds, **subject to** all existing speed, following-distance, intersection and road constraints. No second movement engine.

### 6.1 Input meaning

- Use **Up Arrow / mobile Up driving button** as accelerator for the selected traffic Sedan.
- Holding Up requests bounded positive forward acceleration; letting go removes that acceleration demand and returns selected speed control to ordinary AI/autopilot behaviour (unless brake is currently held). A brief key release must not make the car stop/reverse unexpectedly.
- **Down wins over Up** if both controls are held; no positive/negative conflict or reverse. A full reverse gear is deferred to a separately designed unrestricted driving phase.
- Keep the same control surfaces and camera. Maintain the normal parked GLB driving input contract in parked mode.

### 6.2 Arbitration order (critical)

The driving input modifies a **requested forward speed/acceleration**, not directly `x` or `z`. Reuse the traffic simulator's existing `MAX_SPEED = 8 m/s` and `ACCEL = 2 m/s²` initial bounds and braking deceleration, instead of copying Ferrari `DRIVE_LIMITS` into the traffic subsystem.

Suggested per-tick authority:

1. Compute normal NH-012 candidate movement/connector eligibility for every agent.
2. For the selected agent, resolve driver input precedence: **held brake → stopped target; else held accelerator → forward acceleration demand; else autonomous AI**.
3. Apply unconditional final traffic safety bounds: speed ceiling, remaining distance to stop line, leader gap, junction reservation and connector occupancy.
4. Commit exactly one agent position/heading update at `NH012_SIMULATION_DT`; renderer reads that same state and animates original wheels from travel distance.
5. Non-selected agents still use unmodified AI decisions.

The ordering is conceptual; engineering must place each final clamp at the correct point in the existing two-pass following-gap and connector update algorithm. Manual demand must **never bypass** safeStopSpeed, safeFollowSpeed, or the exclusive junction reservation. Do not permit throttle to drive through the car ahead or a blocked connector. Adjust the chosen agent's motion while preserving invariant checks from `NH012TrafficSimulator.validate()`.

### 6.3 User experience, failure paths and tests

- Driver presses Up → Sedan accelerates smoothly along its current authorised traffic route; wheel rolling reflects the actual travelled distance.
- Driver releases Up → AI resumes bounded movement, without abrupt jump in speed, camera, position or heading.
- Driver presses Down → Stage 2 stop overrides accelerator; holding both follows brake precedence.
- Accelerator at red/blocked intersection equivalent (existing all-way-stop priority), behind a slow vehicle or while reservation held cannot force unlawful progress.
- Preserves traffic rate (20 Hz) and smooth render-camera updates; no rapid per-frame changes to physics owner or vehicle mesh.
- Tests cover step-size stability, braking vs acceleration precedence, hold/release boundaries, following-gap checks, stop-line calculations, incident-free restart of AI, mobile gesture/button semantics and no changes to the other 124 agents.
- **Owner acceptance required before Stage 4.**

Deliverable: The selected Sedan has manual brake and accelerator while its route and road legality remain AI-governed.

## 7. Stage 4 — Steering commands and increasing driver independence

**Owner-approved design intent:** Introduce **Left and Right** steering buttons, initially as requests for **permitted turns on the existing NH-012 road network**. The simulator continues to generate a valid curve, heading, position and wheel turning through the accepted route graph. Do not prematurely attempt GTA-style free steering anywhere in the city.

### 7.1 Bounded manual turn choices

- **Left Arrow / mobile Left** requests the next legal left turn. **Right Arrow / mobile Right** requests the next legal right turn. A no-direction/default state leaves junction choice to established AI.
- Interpret left/right **relative to the Sedan's forward heading**, never to the camera orbit or screen direction.
- A direction request applies at the **next eligible junction**. It must not rotate `heading` or shift lateral `x/z` independently mid-lane. Use one accepted `TrafficConnector012` from `graph.turns.get(agent.edgeId)`, not arbitrary yaw or a fabricated path.
- At the upcoming junction, verify candidate connector is legal (no prohibited U-turn, no missing out-edge), conflict reservation is available, and outgoing lane has space.
- If one requested direction has no legal connector, report "No [left/right] turn available" and maintain the safe AI route; **never** stop indefinitely or turn through buildings merely because a button was pressed.
- Apply a deterministic pending-intent lifecycle: momentary tap queues one turn at the next unresolved junction, repeated changes before route commitment replace the prior selection, and crossing that junction consumes/clears the request. Manual commands cannot retroactively re-route an agent already on a committed connector.
- Document held button semantics so holding Left/Right does **not** unintentionally queue continuous turns through successive intersections unless specifically approved by the owner.
- If the existing `plannedConnectorId` is already chosen, implement explicit replacement/invalidation only before junction reservation/entry, with tests that prevent stale cached turns. Once on a connector, preserve its reservation to completion.

### 7.2 Visual steering and feedback

- Curves still use `nh012PointOnConnector` and `nh012HeadingBetween`; steering animation still derives from `trafficTurnSteering013` and measured Sedan wheel hubs in GEN-VEH-009.
- Avoid double steering from both a new player wheel-angle input and the existing renderer steering calculation. Turn input selects **where the car will go**, not a competing wheel quaternion.
- Show subtle, nonintrusive queued direction and "turn accepted / unavailable" feedback; HUD remains standard across desktop/mobile.
- The car is still lane-constrained and subject to all stopping/following rules. Returning to no manual turn request restores AI junction selection.

### 7.3 Scope boundary: true unrestricted steering is later work

There are **two different technical milestones**:

**Included in Stage 4:** manual legal turn selection while NH-012 continues handling road path, wheel pose, spacing, junctions and traffic.

**Explicitly excluded from Stage 4:** free-angle continuous steering, crossing lane boundaries, weaving around cars, entering parking bays, arbitrary reversing, leaving the lane graph, off-road driving, and full dynamic car-on-car Rapier physics. NH-012 is a directed **lane-parameter** simulator, not an unconstrained chassis collision solver. Achieving free steering would need a separately reviewed off-graph motion/physics safety design, traffic reconciliation and the same source-geometry consistency checks that NH-OPT-011 failed. It is **not** implicitly authorized by this document or Stage 4 acceptance.

### 7.4 Stage 4 acceptance tests

- Left, Right and autonomous/default direction selection on straight, corner and T/cross intersections.
- Accurate heading-relative turn identification independent of camera rotation.
- Invalid turn fallback, no U-turn, no conflicting junction reservation, no wrong-side travel, no crossing pedestrian buildings.
- Queued direction only consumed at the next valid junction; cached route updates correctly; one-shot tap does not drive repeated unintended turns.
- Brake/accelerator can operate through curves without entering an occupied intersection or clipping into a leading vehicle.
- Full Sedan fidelity and mobile frame-time remain comparable to Stage 3; no GLB swap, no detached wheels, 125 agents remain intact.
- Owner manual evaluation after visual preview; no main merge or release without acceptance.

Deliverable: Assisted Sedan driving with manual stop, accelerate and choose-next-turn controls, operating within the single accepted traffic network.

The prior rejected attempt rematerialized native wheels into another chassis hierarchy, sometimes applying a compensating centre translation to meshes that were already source-pivot-centred. This is a **risk-reduction argument**, not an assertion of tested correctness. Real-browser screenshot comparison is mandatory after Stage 1, not merely NullEngine/CI assertions.
